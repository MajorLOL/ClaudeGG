## A phone as a console: the plan

The aim, in the words of the request: *in the setup's list of consoles a phone ("Mobile") is added, and the Raspberry Pi is a microcontroller next to the Pico and the ESP32.* For a Switch, Claude GG looks through a capture card and presses through a board; for a phone the Pi is both, over the network, and **Claude GG does the thinking in C++**, as for every console. The Python bot that came with the repository thinks on the Pi (a Pi 4 with 64-bit OS, LiteRT models from RegiBot, OCR) and stays in [Mobile](../../Mobile) until Claude GG does what it does.

1. **Done: the way to the phone.** The [agent](../../Mobile/agent) on the Pi and its client in Claude GG, with the protocol in its README and a test in every build.
2. **Done: the setup.** The phone is the third tile on the screen with the consoles. It has no board to pick: the Raspberry Pi is the microcontroller, and a page of its own comes in place of the boards, with the steps (USB debugging on the phone, `git clone` and the installer on the Pi), the address, the port, the token and "Test the connection", which says what phone it found. The token is kept apart from the settings, sealed for the Windows account like the assistant's keys. Left for it: showing a picture of the phone in that test. Issue #28.
3. **Done: the picture.** `GET /v1/video` of the agent is a stream instead of one screenshot at a time (a screenshot takes 1 to 3 seconds): `screenrecord` through `ffmpeg` on the Pi, about ten JPEG pictures a second, one pipeline for all who look. `MobileVideo` in Claude GG keeps the newest picture. Tested in every build with a fake picture; the real pipeline (H.264 through ffmpeg) is tested against a fake `adb` in the agent's own tests; not yet seen on a real phone. Issue #29.
4. **The screen readers in C++:** the three models of [RegiBot](https://github.com/Juancavr6/RegiBot) (MIT; which screen, where the Pokémon, stops and buttons are, the throw's ring) run with a runtime that works on Windows (LiteRT or ONNX Runtime; Pokémon Automation already uses ONNX Runtime for other readers), plus the text reading the bot does with Tesseract. Issue #30. **Started:** seven parts of the Battle League are ported to C++ and held, in every build, to what the Python code answers (the golden files are made by `Mobile/tools/make_*_golden.py`, a test of Python says when one is old): the text side (`battle_nav.py`: which screen it is, where to tap, what a question or a result says; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoNav`, 6331 answers on 109 screens), the brain of the fights (`battle_engine.py`: damage, matchups, shields, which charged attack to throw, when to switch; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoBattle`, 14,688 answers), the state of a fight (`battle_fight.py`: 60 fights, 14,913 calls; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoFight`) and the readers of the picture (`battle_vision.py`: name boxes, Poké Balls, shields, HP bars, ready buttons, the banner, the countdown, the buttons of the screens around a fight; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoVision` with OpenCV, 696 pictures) and the little game of a charged attack (`battle_minigame.py`: where the type icons are on a picture and which swipes to make over a stream or a row of them; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoMinigame`, 370 pictures and 243 games of 26,212 steps; the Python side was made deterministic first, as NumPy leaves the sense of the main axis of a group of icons to its linear algebra library). Also ported: the models that read the game (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoModels`): the screen classifier, the map, the clickable-object and the encounter detector and the throw predictor, run with the ONNX Runtime that the program already has (`Mobile/tools/convert_models_to_onnx.py` makes ONNX copies of the TFLite files and the build checks them against the originals), with the same picture preparation, anchor decoding and non-maximum suppression as `gamebot.py`; held, in every build, to what the TFLite models say on 110 pictures, 18 made-up answers of a network (with many equal certainties, so that the order of equal ones counts) and 200 throws; the encounter detector ends in an operation that ONNX has no name for, so its network is cut in front of it and the post-processing (box decoding, best class, the fast non-maximum suppression of TensorFlow Lite) is done in C++ (`Mobile/tools/make_models_golden.py`; the files are fetched from RegiBot and checked against their SHA-256, they are not in the repository). The loop that ties it together is ported too (`fight_battle` of `gamebot.py`; `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoLoop`): the live picture (or screenshots when there is none), the finger that taps the fast attack on its own and sweeps over the little game, the reader of the banner and the names that runs next to it, the questions (*Exit the Trainer Battle?*, the shield question), giving up and the result. It is held, in every build, to 51 fights that the Python loop played with a fake clock and a fake phone (`Mobile/tools/make_loop_golden.py`: the fights of its tests and seven of its own, where a number of the loop has its limit; the numbers themselves are held to those of `gamebot.py` as well): the C++ loop is shown the same pictures and text, in the same order, and must make the same taps, swipes, log lines and sleeps at the same moments (30,009 events, no difference), and the check of whether the map is in view is held to the Python answer on every test picture. No program uses them yet. The text reader is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoText`): it calls the `tesseract` program as `gamebot.py` does (the reader inside the program, tesseractPA, gives text but not the place of the words, and the screens are read by their places) and makes lines of its table; held, in every build, to what `gamebot.py` makes of 354 tables (`Mobile/tools/make_text_golden.py`: 36 read from frames of real fights, the rest made up and random), and on 120 real readings the same words came out at the same places. The phone side of the loop is there too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoPhone`): `AgentHost` is the loop's `Host` over the agent of the Pi - the live picture (`/v1/video`, made 704 x 880 as in `gamebot.py`, each picture with the moment it came in), the screenshot on the 1768 x 2208 canvas, the fingers (a press, a quick tap, finger down and up as two events - for that `/v1/tap` of the agent takes a `kind`, so the agent on the Pi has to be updated - and swipes in commands of 50, on the real screen with the rounding of `spin_stop.to_real`), the text reader and the files of what the bot learns (`battle_seen.json`, `battle_minigame.json`, in the format of `gamebot.py`). It is tested in every build, against the agent with a made-up phone (the touches arrive as the loop asks for them, a phone that is off is told in the log, the pictures of the video come with their time) and without one (the pictures, the coordinates, the files). The walk around a fight is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoFlow`: `battle_open_menu`, `_battle_to_map` and `battle_run` of `gamebot.py`, with `_settled`, `screen_changed` and the label of a button): from the map or the Battle screen to the party screen of the league (the main menu, the claim of a new rank and the screens after it, the gold reward tiles of a finished set, a new set that has to be scrolled to, the welcome of a new season, the list of leagues, pop-ups with a green button that only goes on, never one that buys, leaves or gives something away), back to the map without ever pressing BACK in a running battle, and a run of battles one after the other. It is held, in every build, to 133 stories that the Python walk played with a fake clock and a fake phone (`Mobile/tools/make_flow_golden.py`: what it was shown - screenshots, readings of the text reader, the answers of the check for the map and of the screen classifier, the fights - and what it did, 9,482 events, no difference; the picture that is given to the text reader for the label of a button is held to the one of `gamebot.py` pixel by pixel, and the numbers of the walk to those of `gamebot.py`). The start of the game is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoStart`: `restart_game` of `spin_stop.py` with `startup_screen`, the news card and the safety notice of the start, the two kinds of green button, the settings of the battles and `auto_battle`): the game is closed and started again, a screenshot is looked at every couple of seconds, a news card or the notice is tapped away the moment it shows up, and the start ends when the map has been there twice in a row. It is held, in every build, to 424 pictures and 72 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_start_golden.py`; 1490 events, no difference; the colours, sizes and distances of the two button finders to the last step). `AgentHost` is the host of the walk and the start too: the clock of the day, BACK, the screen classifier (the ONNX models, when they are there), closing and starting the app through the new `/v1/app` of the agent (so the agent on the Pi has to be updated for that, as for the `kind` of a tap), the day of the battles that are done (`battle_done.json`, as `gamebot.py` writes it) and hooks for what only the program knows (the count of the day, the team, the time of the run). The team of the fights is ported as well (`Battle::load_team`: `battle_team.json` in the folder of the bot, the preset when there is none, an error for a file that is JSON but no team, as `battle_engine.load_team` has it, down to what Python takes for true and what `int()` makes of a text; 98 files of the engine golden of `Mobile/tools/make_engine_golden.py`), and `AgentHost::load_team()` hands it to `Start::auto_battle`. The step on the map is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoBrain` and `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoMap`: `do_map` and `do_pokestop` of `gamebot.py` and `spin_stop.py` with the looks at the map, the second look, the camera that turns while the next picture is taken, a fast catch that is checked on the map, the spin of a stop and the bag that is cleaned when it is full, and the four readers of pixels). It is held, in every build, to 197 pictures and 328 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_map_golden.py`; 7,736 events, no difference; the parts that are not ported yet - the encounter, the gifts, the bag clean-up itself - are seams that the story plays as events), and the test was tested with about 380 deliberate faults in the port, of which all but seven (that change nothing a test could see) make it fail. The readers and sums of the throw are ported as well (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoThrow`: the size of the coloured ring next to the white one, the flying ball and where it came down, the buttons and the result screen of a catch, the shrink speed of the ring, what the bot learns from its own throws, the lean and the spin of a curveball), held to 212 rings, 103 balls, 141 buttons, 48 speeds and 106 timings of `Mobile/tools/make_throw_golden.py` down to the last bit (the C++ does the sums of NumPy where the Python code leans on them); the test was tested with 354 deliberate faults, of which 334 make it fail and the other 20 change nothing a test could see. The throw itself is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoThrowPlay`: `throw_ball` and `throw_touch` - one throw as a single touch: the finger goes down and spins the ball, the ring is followed with screenshots or the live video, the throw is timed so that the ring has the size that is wanted, the swipe is cut into steps of the delay that the phone has, the ball is let go, and what the flight and the size of the ring at the release teach - with `humanize`, `aim`, `ring_track`, `watch_flight` and the file of what the bot learned): held, in every build, to 346 stories that the Python code played with a fake clock and a fake phone (`Mobile/tools/make_throwplay_golden.py`: what it was shown - screenshots, pictures of the live video, boxes of the detector, the answer of the throw predictor, the numbers that were drawn, a phone command that fails - and what it did, every command, wait, log line and file written, with the time of each; the stories play every line and every branch of those functions), and the test was tested with deliberate faults in the port, one at a time (a number off by one, a `<` for a `<=`, a line left out): every one that no story caught got a story of its own or changes nothing a test could see. The catch screen around the throw is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoCatch`: `do_encounter` with the choice of ball, the berry that is picked from the tray (the table of the text reader, read as `gamebot.py` reads it), the limit of throws, the throw loop with the fast catch, `wait_for_result`, `fast_catch_leave`, `finish_catch` and the OK of the result screen), held, in every build, to 417 stories that the Python code played with a fake clock and a fake phone (`Mobile/tools/make_catch_golden.py`: 3,281 events, no difference; the stories play 100 % of the lines and branches of those eleven functions, and the test was tested with 412 + 117 deliberate faults in the port, of which every one that no story caught got a story of its own or changes nothing a test could see); `ClaudeGG_PokemonGoStory.h` is the check-only `Host` that plays the events of such a story, for this part and the ones that follow. The step that picks what to do on each screen is ported as well (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoStep`: `step()` of `gamebot.py` with `heat_wait`, `leave_group_screen`, `is_rocket_text`, `storage_full` and `pause_catching`: the heat, the shape of the screen, the phone that is awake, the screenshot that was handed on, away from the map or stuck: start the game again, a tap on a Pokémon that did nothing, Team GO Rocket, the first looks at the map, and then the map, the stop, the catch screen, an egg, the summary, a news card, the group question, the safety notice, a full storage, the passenger pop-up and the result screen), held, in every build, to 269 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_step_golden.py`: 3,358 events, no difference; 100 % of the lines and branches of those functions; the test was tested with 368 deliberate faults in the port, of which every one that no story caught got a story of its own or changes nothing a test could see); what it hands on - the map, the stop and the catch screen, the layout check, the lock screen, the quests and the Team Rocket battle - are seams of its host. The gifts are ported as well (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoGifts`: the friend list that is read - the names, the pink bow of a gift that waits and the grey arrow of one that was sent -, the readers of the gift screens, the memory of the day (one file for each Pokémon GO app, a new day starts empty), a visit to one friend (open the gift that waits, scroll the profile in small steps to SEND GIFT, look at the colour of the icon, choose the first gift, SEND) and the whole round with the list scrolled to its end), held, in every build, to 385 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_gifts_golden.py`: 5,890 events, no difference; the stories play every line and branch of those functions; the test was tested with 613 deliberate faults in the port, of which 17 do not compile, 585 of the other 596 make it fail and the other 11 change nothing a test could see). The fight of Team GO Rocket itself is ported as well (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoRocketFight`: `rocket_fight` of `gamebot.py` - the fast taps on one charged attack button and then the other, the shield for every shield question, the name of ours that is read every 6 seconds and gives the types of its charged attacks, the little game of a charged attack that is swiped when three icons of one of those types show up, and the end: the HUD gone for 10 seconds, 300 seconds, or no fight in the first 40 seconds), held, in every build, to 39 stories of the Python code on real pictures of fights and little games (`Mobile/tools/make_rocketfight_golden.py`: 11,961 events, no difference; 100 % of the lines and branches; the test was tested with 166 deliberate faults in the port, of which 10 do not compile, 135 make it fail and the other 21 change nothing a test could see). The daily run is next. Left: a trial with a real phone and a real Tesseract, and the programs that use all this (issue #31).
5. **The programs, first version: done.** The game "Pokémon GO" (shown for the phone only; the games of the Switch are not) has seven programs of the day's play: Spin PokéStops, Catch Pokémon, Spin PokéStops and catch Pokémon, Gifts, Daily run, Team GO Rocket battles and Claim GO Pass rewards (the last two marked experimental). Each is a preset of the Python bot on the Pi: it turns the bot's switches on or off (with the throw, the daily start and the other options of the bot as options of the program), starts it through the agent (`/v1/bot`: `systemctl` through a sudoers rule for `start`, `stop` and `restart` of the bot's service only, which `install-agent.sh` writes), writes what the bot logs into the output log of the program, and stops the bot when the program is stopped. The page with the programs shows the live picture of the phone instead of a capture card. Tested in every build against the agent with a service that is not one (the presets' switches are accepted by the agent, the service is started and stopped, a wrong token starts nothing); not yet tried on a real phone. Left: the same programs done in C++ (issue #31), which needs the screen readers (#30).
6. **The other parts of the bot, first version: done (build 0.2.0).** Three programs more. **GO Battle League** runs a set of battles (the league, how many, which of the Pokémon GO apps, and whether a lost fight is given up), and the Daily run can fight battles first, as the bot does. **Your Pokémon and teams** runs a step of the bot's collection: the leagues, a tag per league, the scan of the storage (all, the last week, the first five), shadow, lucky and purified, the teams, their moves and candy, the tags, or the whole weekly round; the Daily run can keep the teams up to date once a week. **One part of the bot** runs any one of the 27 parts that *Test* in the bot's web interface has (what the bot sees, one battle, the Rocket fight, one throw, the bag, ...). They go through three new endpoints of the agent (`/v1/bot/tests`, `/v1/bot/test`, `/v1/bot/test/stop`), and the number of matches won and lost today comes with the bot's state. Tested in every build against the agent with a bot that is not one; the list of parts that the programs know is compared with the bot's own `chapters.py`. Still the Python bot that plays: the bot's own record on the phone is thin ("Battles are not proven yet": two of its eight real fights were won), so it is all experimental. The bot's web interface keeps what is not a program: the history of the days, the warnings to a phone (ntfy), the backup, updating from GitHub, and the chat with Claude.

   *Online battles:* this was left out until build 0.1.0.10 (the reason was the one above for Pokémon Champions: people on the other side). 7. **Claude GG plays itself (started, 28 November 2026).** GO Battle League can be played by the C++ port (the option *Who plays*; the Pi then only gives the picture and the taps, agent 0.2.0 and later). **Auto play** (build after 0.2.0) is the bot's normal play with each part chosen: spin, catch, gifts, Team GO Rocket, GO Pass, with all the options of the catch, the bag and the look. *Spin PokéStops*, *Catch Pokémon*, *Spin PokéStops and catch Pokémon*, *Gifts*, *Claim GO Pass rewards* and *Auto play* (with spin, catch, gifts, Team GO Rocket and the GO Pass) have *Who plays* too (experimental, the bot of the Pi stays the default): Claude GG runs the ported `step()`, the map, the stop and the catch screen with the ported throw over the agent (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoBot`; it needs the ONNX models in the folder of the bot and Tesseract, and agent 0.2.2 for catching: its `/v1/touch` holds, spins and lets go of the ball as single touch events); what is not ported yet is switched off so that it leaves the game alone (the camera swipe is waited for, no live video for looking or throwing yet). The ported gifts (open the ones that wait, send one to every friend; the memory of the day is `gifts_state.json` in the folder of the bot) and the ported clean-up of a full bag (with the red FULL marks of the main menu) are connected to it too: the keys and the closing of the game that the bag needs go to the agent (`/v1/key`, `/v1/app`), and when the storage and the bag are both full and the game has been closed the bot stops with a line in the log, because the agent cannot say whether the game is open again (`pidof`), which `gamebot.py` waits for. The ported GO Pass rewards (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoQuests`: the quest button that turns gold, the tab GO PASS, its pages, VIEW REWARDS, CLAIM ALL, a Pokémon as a reward that is caught by the normal steps, the X that closes it; looked at most every 15 minutes, an hour after a look that found nothing) are connected to it as well, held to 112 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_quests_golden.py`) and tried with the harness only, not on a phone. **Team GO Rocket** is connected to it too (the option *Team GO Rocket* of Auto play and the program *Team GO Rocket battles* now have *Who plays*): `PhoneBot` is the `Rocket::Host` of the ported walk (`rocket_battle`) and fight (`rocket_fight`), which look over the live video of the agent when there is one; experimental, held to the golden files of the Python code only, not tried on a phone. **The Daily run** is connected to it as well (the program *Daily run* now has *Who plays*, with its start time, its time limit and the app and options for the battles): `PhoneBot` is the `Daily::Host` of the ported `daily_cycle` and `run_one_game` (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoDaily`: the start time of the day, the start that a run uses up, the apps one after another in the order of the agent's list - the Play Store one first -, the battles first, the time played and booked, the save every minute and the report every half hour, a Pokémon of a reward that is caught and then the battles again, a catch that is finished when the time is up, the game closed at the end), held to 156 stories of the Python code (`Mobile/tools/make_daily_golden.py`, every ported line covered, the golden checked by mutating the C++ code) and tried with the harness and a fake agent only, not on a phone. It needs agent 0.2.3 (`/v1/app` with `list`; an older agent is refused with a line that says what to update) and the memory of the day is `gamebot_daily.json` in the folder of the bot on this computer (the bot of the Pi keeps its own file, the two do not share a day). What differs from the Python code: there is no watchdog (that is a thread of the Pi: a step that hangs is not stopped by force here, the Stop button is), a step that fails is logged and waited out for ten seconds where the Python code would stop the bot and let systemd start it again, the weekly refresh of the teams is not ported (it is never due), and a start time with more than nine digits is taken as the standard 08:00. **Niantic's Terms of Service forbid this kind of automation: use a spare account.** **The pause for the heat** is played now (agent 0.2.4: `GET /v1/heat`, the battery of the phone from `dumpsys battery` and the processor of the Pi from `/sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone0/temp`; the ported `heat_wait` pauses from 43 °C of the phone or 82 °C of the Pi until it is below 39 / 72 °C, at most 45 minutes); with an older agent the option is switched off and the log says so. Tried with the harness and a fake agent only. **The lock screen and the dimmed game** are ported as well (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoLock`, held to 178 stories of the Python code in `Mobile/tools/make_lock_golden.py`, the golden checked by mutating the C++ code): the swipe lock screen of the phone is dismissed with `wm dismiss-keyguard`, swiped away and reported when it stays (a PIN or a pattern is not taken away), and a game that dims and locks itself (Samsung's Game Booster: the brightest 0.5 % of the picture below 120) is tapped and its little lock dragged up, to the right and down until the picture is bright again. The bot asks whether the phone is awake and locked once a minute or when the last look did not show a game screen, as `gamebot.py` does. It needs agent 0.2.5 (`GET /v1/power`, `POST /v1/unlock`); with an older agent the phone is taken as awake and unlocked, and the log says so once. Tried with the harness and a fake agent only. **The layout check** is ported too (`ClaudeGG_PokemonGoLayout`, held to 276 stories of the Python code in `Mobile/tools/make_layout_golden.py`, checked by mutating the C++ code): the shape of the screen is judged (within 10 % of the 1768 x 2208 canvas), the Poké Ball menu button at the bottom of the map is looked for and compared with where the canvas expects it, and when the game is not drawn the way the bot expects, Claude GG lets Android draw it in the right shape (`wm size`: the phone then shows black bars) after writing down on the Pi what it changes, taps the menu button to see that the menu opens, and undoes everything that does not work; the size is put back when the run ends, and a change that an earlier run did not put back is taken over. **This changes the display size of the phone while Claude GG plays** (`adb shell wm size reset` puts it back by hand); a file `~/gamebot_no_display_change` on the Pi switches that off, and `~/gamebot_allow_unsupported` plays on a screen of any shape. It needs agent 0.2.6 (`GET`/`POST /v1/display`); with an older agent the shape of the screen is not checked, and the log says so. Where it differs from the Python code: the screen is looked at through the agent (its size and model) and not with `screencap` of every display, so a foldable with two displays plays on the one that the agent was started with (its option `--display`). As in `main()` of `gamebot.py` the loop looks at the screen before every cycle, the daily run included: on a screen that cannot be played on the bot adapts the display or waits, and does not start the cycle (harness test: a run on a 1080x2340 screen with the changing switched off, normal and daily, says why it waits, ends with the stop of the person and sends no tap). Tried with the harness and a fake agent only. Next: the incense and the leftovers.

   *Pokémon GO on an iPhone:* not possible with the boards of now. The iPhone route is a keyboard (the ESP32-S3 as HID: Keyboard, with Full Keyboard Access): it cannot tap a place on the screen or swipe, and the focus frame of Full Keyboard Access does not go into a Unity game. A possible way for later is a mouse and keyboard through AssistiveTouch, as the open project [esp32-iphone-agent](https://github.com/onehumanbeing/esp32-iphone-agent) does (an ESP32-S3 as mouse and keyboard, the picture through AirPlay with UxPlay); its license is not checked yet, and the firmware of Pokémon Automation's boards cannot do it. The owner decides whether to try it.

Automating Pokémon GO is against Niantic's Terms of Service; accounts can be warned, suspended or banned. Everything on the phone is experimental, says so on its screens, and is used at the user's own risk.
